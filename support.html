Support

A question, a mistake in a lesson, an ad to report? Write to us: bpennes@gmail.com. Tell us the level, lesson or question (for example “Open Water · L11 · question 3”).

Common questions

My progress is gone

It is saved on your iPhone and follows its backups (iCloud or computer). Reinstalling without a backup starts from scratch.

I bought Octo Pro on another device

Open Settings › Octo Pro › Restore Purchases, with the same Apple Account.

Meters or feet?

Octo uses your region’s units. Change them in Settings › Units.

An answer looks wrong

Write to us and quote the question: every report is checked, and the fix comes with the next update. When in doubt, your instructor’s word comes first.
